✉️ SPF / DMARC / DKIM Lookup

Check whether a domain can be spoofed by email. Looks up SPF (with recursive DNS-lookup counting), DMARC, DKIM, MX and MTA-STS, then scores and explains the gaps.

Domain

Privacy: lookups use public DNS-over-HTTPS (Google Public DNS, falling back to Cloudflare), so the domain name you enter is sent to those resolvers from your browser. Nothing goes to this site's server (there isn't one). DKIM selectors can't be enumerated: leave blank to try ~15 common ones, or enter the exact selector from an email's DKIM-Signature: s= tag.

How scoring works

Starts at 100. Missing SPF or DMARC are critical (-30 each). DMARC p=none, +all/?all, more than 10 SPF lookups, multiple records and weak DKIM keys deduct further. A domain without enforced DMARC (quarantine/reject) is reported as spoofable. This only inspects DNS: it cannot confirm your mail servers actually sign or align correctly. Check real message headers for that.
