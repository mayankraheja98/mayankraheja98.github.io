🛡 CSP Evaluator

Paste a Content-Security-Policy (header value, full header line, or <meta> tag). Get a score, the gaps, and fixes. Runs entirely in your browser.

Policy

How scoring works

Starts at 100 and deducts points per finding (critical/high gaps in script control cost the most). If scripts are not effectively restricted, the score is capped at 49, since the policy will not stop XSS. Report-Only policies are capped at 40. This is a static heuristic, not a substitute for testing: it can't see your app, server-side nonce generation or other headers.

Recommended baseline (strict, nonce-based)
Replace {RANDOM} with a fresh random nonce per response and add the nonce to every legitimate <script> tag.
